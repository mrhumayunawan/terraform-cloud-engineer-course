# Exam Registration

---

## Step-01: Exam Registration

- The exam is conducted on the **PSI Exam platform**.
  
### Pre-requisites:
1. **Pre-requisite-1**: Ensure you have an account on **github.com**. The email associated with your GitHub account will be used for communication by HashiCorp via PSI Exams, so make sure you have access to that email.
2. **Pre-requisite-2**: Create an account on [**YourAcclaim**](https://www.youracclaim.com/) using the same email address. Once you complete the exam and become certified, your badge will be posted to **YourAcclaim**, allowing you to share it on social media platforms like LinkedIn and have an online record of your achievement.
  
- [**Register for Exam**](https://hashicorp-certifications.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049382552)
- After registering, you will receive an email confirmation.

---

## Step-02: Review Exam Taker Handbook

- It's important to go through the [**Exam Taker Handbook**](https://hashicorp-certifications.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360048211571) to understand the exam process, expectations, and guidelines.

---

## Step-03: System Requirements

- **Very important**: Make sure to set everything up a day before the exam.
  
- Check out the [**System Requirements**](https://hashicorp-certifications.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360048446631) and ensure your system meets the necessary specifications.

- [**Click here to test your system's compatibility**](https://syscheck.bridge.psiexams.com/)

- If you're on **Mac**, you'll need to grant additional permissions for the system check. Here are some additional things to keep in mind:
  - **Webcam**: Connect a webcam or use your laptop's built-in camera.
  - **Microphone**: Ensure your microphone is connected (no headphones allowed).
  - **Internet connection**: Use a wired ethernet connection for better speed. As a fail-safe, enable your mobile hotspot outside your room to avoid distractions.
  - **Install PSI Secure Browser**: Required for the exam.

---

## Step-04: Exam Day

- **Login**: Log in to the **PSI Exam platform** at least 45 minutes before your scheduled exam time. The **Exam Launch URL** will be available 30 minutes before the exam.

### Step-04-01: First Time Checks
- The system will ask for the following checks:
  1. **Identity Card Verification**
  2. **360-Degree Room View**
  3. **Hand Sleeves and Ears Check**

### Step-04-02: Second Time Checks
- An **Online Proctor** will communicate with you via chat and ask for the following checks:
  1. **Identity Card Verification**
  2. **360-Degree Room View**
  3. **Hand Sleeves and Ears Check**

### Step-04-03: Proctor Will Release the Exam for You to Start
- Don’t be nervous, take a deep breath!
- Start answering questions once the proctor releases the exam.
- **Flagging Questions**: If you're unsure about any question, click on the **Flag Button** so you can come back to it later.
  - **Tip**: Avoid flagging too many questions to make it easier to review later.
- **Exam Time**: 60 Minutes, **57 Questions**.
- **Time Estimate**: Most of the questions (40 to 45) will be straightforward. I completed my exam in **35 minutes**.
- After answering, review any flagged questions and answer them.
- Once you complete the exam, fill out the **Survey** questions.
- You’ll receive a status update on whether you passed or failed the exam.
- An email will be sent to you by **YourAcclaim** with your badge information.
- **HashiCorp** will send an email with your score report.

---

Good luck! You’ve got this!
